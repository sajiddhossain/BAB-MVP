This is about learning to read yourself

Most sports apps measure you from the outside and tell you what to do. BAB does the opposite: it helps you learn what your own body is telling you — so you know when to push, when to rest, and how to say it out loud.

✅ What this is

  • About a minute, twice on a training day
  • A skill you build, that stays yours
  • Yours — you choose what to share

🚫 What this isn't

  • A score that judges you
  • Anything to do with food or weight
  • Something your coach can watch

Step 4 · Your rhythm

Do you have your period yet?

If you do, BAB can use it as the backdrop behind all your other signals — the thing almost no sports app does. It's yours, it stays yours, and you can switch it off any time.

When did your last periods start?

Best guess is fine. Even one date lets BAB start; two or three make it better. At your age cycles are often still settling, so BAB treats this as a rough map, never a schedule.

Are you on any hormonal contraception?

Pill, implant, coil, patch, injection. About half of athletes are, and it changes what your signals mean — so BAB needs to know to read them properly.

Why BAB asks for dates instead of "which phase are you in?"
Because working it out from dates is the reliable way — guessing your own phase isn't, especially in the first years. You'll never have to classify yourself.
🔒 This is the most private thing here. Your cycle is never shared with a coach, never shown to anyone, and never included in anything you send — unless you tick it yourself, every single time.
