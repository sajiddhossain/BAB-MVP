Decode any ache

Anything show up during or after?

Some things only appear once you stop. Just name the sensation — deciding what to do comes next.

✅ Working & everyday ache

  • The burn or tiredness of muscles that worked hard
  • Muscles feeling tight or stiff
  • Soreness that's fairly even on both sides
  • Dull, come-and-go aches — a crampy tummy or lower back, or a heavy, bloated feeling

🛡️ Protective pain

  • Sharp, stabbing or sudden
  • Inside a joint or a bone
  • On one side only, or makes you limp
  • New, and not easing as you cool down

Step 4 · Recover — what your body's asking for now

    Every time you compare your morning read to what actually happened, your predictions get a little sharper. That's the whole game — you're learning to trust yourself. 🌱

    Recovery isn't the boring bit after training — it's where the training actually works. Listening to what your body asks for now is you being your own best coach. For protective pain or anything that feels off, always loop in your coach, physio or parents.
